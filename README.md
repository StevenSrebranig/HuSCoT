What Is HuSCoT?

HuSCoT unifies eight analytical methods into a single, interoperable system:

CSA — Coherence mapping across five layers

WSA — Structural load analysis

CSM — Pressure-field dynamics

WCS/DR — Cohesion, entropy, and risk modeling

SECL — Stability and feedback envelopes

HCM — Drift detection

MEO/OO — Opportunity and equilibrium modeling

HJC — Interface–substrate distinction

HuSCoT offers a shared language for understanding how systems hold together, destabilize, and recover.

Key Capabilities

Coherence analysis across narrative, organizational, cognitive, and social systems

Detection of fragmentation, overload, rising entropy, and drift

Early-warning signals for instability

Interoperable metrics that flow cleanly between modules

Actionable insights for stabilization and redesign

Interpretable math designed for human and AI-assisted analysis
